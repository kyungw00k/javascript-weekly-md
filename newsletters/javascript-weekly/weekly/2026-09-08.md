[How Safari Fixed Top-Level `await`](https://webkit.org/blog/18227/fixing-top-level-await-in-safari/) — Safari's module loader was originally built on an abandoned spec proposal and adding top-level `await` caused years of *"cannot access before initialization"* errors. Safari 27 brings full spec compliance for it, but it took a ground-up rewrite and this post explains the complexity.

Kai Tamkun (WebKit)
